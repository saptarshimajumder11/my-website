astraPAD

ASTRApad is a compact custom control pad I'm designing and building as a hardware project. It combines programmable buttons, a rotary control, a small display, and a microcontroller into a single desktop device. The goal is to create a useful mini control center for things like shortcuts, media, settings, and other PC functions while learning about electronics, PCB design, and embedded systems.

ASTRA deskbot

DeskBot is a small interactive desktop robot I'm building with an ESP32, display, and other electronic components. It will feature animated eyes, a responsive interface, and interactive controls, giving it a simple robotic personality while also exploring ideas around voice interaction, automation, and embedded systems.
